Time remaining for each agenda item, with meeting notes

Paste your agenda

Durations, clock ranges, CSV and spreadsheet tables. Review the interpretation before starting. Start now or at a specific date and time.

Control the time

Pause, next item, +1/+5 minutes and overtime. The timer has a compact view and a switchable “Always on top” option.

Capture outcomes

Item-specific notes, decisions and tasks with owners and due dates. Export selected content to Markdown or PDF.

A small window to share

Share the timer and current item title. This view contains no notes or transcripts. System/light/dark themes and four interface languages: Polish, English, German and French.

Control the agenda while presenting

While the app is running, global shortcuts change items and add time without returning to the presenter panel. Defaults: Ctrl+Alt+Shift plus B/N/P/U/I/O for previous/next, start/pause, +1 minute, +5 minutes and a custom increment. Change or disable bindings and set a custom increment from 1 to 120 minutes. On macOS Ctrl means Cmd.

Global backends: Windows, macOS and Linux X11. Wayland portal support is still to be implemented; window shortcuts remain available there. Before presenting, check registration status in the app — the OS may block an occupied combination.

Get started

  1. Download a ready-to-run package from GitHub Releases. Choose the app asset for your OS rather than the “Source code” archive.
  2. Extract the entire archive. On Windows run the EXE at the top of the folder; on macOS open the .app. Python is not required. See the package’s README.txt for details.
  3. Accept the terms, paste the agenda and review it. Open the timer and start the meeting.

Audio, screenshots and optional transcription

The full variant adds local audio recording and selected-window capture. Save screen/window PNG images at your chosen interval. Video recording is outside the app’s scope.

OpenAI transcription starts only after you enter your own key and separately confirm uploading the selected audio. The timer and notes work without it. API charges may apply. System audio on macOS requires an available loopback source.

Privacy and support

Agendas, notes, recordings and images stay local. Obtain the required participant consent and permissions before recording or capturing images. Optional transcription sends audio to an external provider; see the app privacy policy (Polish).
