# Terms of use

Meeting timer runs locally. The timer and notes do not require an account.
Recording, screenshots and transcription are optional.

## Participants' consent

**By recording audio or taking screenshots of a screen/window, you confirm
that you have participants' consent to capture the meeting in that scope
and all required permissions.** Before using these features, inform participants
about the purpose and scope. Do not use them without the required consent
or permissions.

Screenshots may capture private messages, personal data and other applications.
You choose the source and are responsible for what you capture, store and share.
System permission to access a microphone or screen does not replace
participants' consent.

## API transcription

Transcription needs your own key and a separate confirmation to send audio
to OpenAI. By uploading a recording, you also confirm you may share it with
that provider. API charges are billed to your account. Cancelling does not
undo uploaded data or possible charges. Agenda, notes and screenshots
are not uploaded.

## Data and reliability

Local files are not automatically encrypted. Protect your device and files.
Exports and chosen folders may be included in your backups or synchronisation.
This application is a prototype: back up important data and do not rely
solely on a recording or transcript.
